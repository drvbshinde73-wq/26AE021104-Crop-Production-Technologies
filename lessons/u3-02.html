Many crops, many branches

Horticulture is a large family. A mango orchard, a tomato field, a rose polyhouse, a coconut plantation and a turmeric farm are all horticulture – but each needs its own knowledge. So horticulture is divided into branches.

2.2 Classification of fruit crops

  • By climate: tropical (mango, banana, papaya), subtropical (citrus, pomegranate, guava, grape), temperate (apple, pear, peach).
  • By life span: perennial (mango), semi-perennial (banana), short-lived (papaya).
  • By fruit type: berry (grape, tomato), drupe (mango, coconut), pome (apple), hesperidium (citrus), syconus (fig).
  • By ripening behaviour: climacteric (mango, banana, tomato – ripen after harvest) and non-climacteric (grape, citrus, pomegranate – must ripen on the plant).

2.3 Classification of vegetable crops

BasisGroups and examples
Part usedLeafy (spinach), root (carrot, radish), tuber (potato), bulb (onion), fruit (tomato, brinjal), pod (bean), flower (cauliflower)
SeasonWarm-season (tomato, okra, cucurbits), cool-season (cabbage, cauliflower, peas)
FamilySolanaceous (tomato, brinjal, chilli), cucurbits, cole crops (Brassicaceae), bulb crops (Alliaceae), legumes

2.4 Classification of flowers and others

  • Flowers: loose flowers (marigold, jasmine, chrysanthemum), cut flowers (rose, gerbera, carnation), pot plants, bulbous (tuberose, gladiolus).
  • Plantation crops: beverage (tea, coffee), nut (coconut, cashew, arecanut), industrial (rubber).
  • MAP: medicinal (ashwagandha, aloe, tulsi) and aromatic (lemongrass, palmarosa, mint, vetiver).

Facts & amazing information

1

Tomato is botanically a fruit (berry) but is grown and used as a vegetable.

2

Banana is a ‘herb’ – the tallest herbaceous plant – not a tree.

3

Coconut is called ‘Kalpavriksha’ – every part of the palm is useful.

4

Climacteric fruits produce a burst of ethylene and respiration during ripening.

Possible ESE questions

Short-answer questions

  1. What is pomology? (2 marks)

    Answer points: Branch of horticulture dealing with fruit crops.

  2. Define olericulture. (2 marks)

    Answer points: Science of growing vegetable crops.

  3. Give two examples of climacteric fruits. (2 marks)

    Answer points: Mango, banana (also tomato, papaya).

Long-answer questions

  1. Describe the branches of horticulture with examples. (5 marks)

    Answer points: Pomology, olericulture, floriculture, landscape, plantation, spices, MAP, post-harvest, nursery; examples.

  2. Classify fruit and vegetable crops on different bases. (10 marks)

    Answer points: Fruits – climate, life span, fruit type, ripening; vegetables – part used, season, family; examples.
