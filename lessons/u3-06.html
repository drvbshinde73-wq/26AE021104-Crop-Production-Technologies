Every drop, every root

Horticultural crops are high-value but thirsty. Supplying the right amount of water at the right place – the root zone – and covering the soil with mulch can save half the water and raise yields.

6.1 Methods of irrigation

MethodHow water is appliedSuitable cropsWater-use efficiency
Flood / check basinWater flooded in basinsOld orchards, riceLow (40–50 %)
Ring and basinRing around the trunk, water in basinFruit treesModerate
FurrowWater in furrows between ridgesVegetables, sugarcane50–60 %
SprinklerWater sprayed like rainLeafy vegetables, lawns, nurseries70–80 %
Drip / trickleWater dripped at root zone through emittersBanana, grape, pomegranate, vegetables90–95 %
Micro-sprinkler / foggerFine spray under canopy / in polyhouseOrchards, greenhouses80–90 %

Explained simply

Drip irrigation is like an IV drip in a hospital – small, steady doses right where they are needed.

6.2 Drip irrigation and fertigation

  • Components: pump, sand/screen filters, fertilizer tank/venturi, main and sub-main, laterals, drippers (2–8 L/h).
  • Saves 40–60 % water, 25–30 % fertilizer; increases yield 20–50 %; fewer weeds; suits saline water and undulating land.
  • Fertigation – applying water-soluble fertilizers through drip in small doses (19:19:19, urea, potassium nitrate).
  • Limitations: high initial cost (subsidy under PMKSY), clogging – needs acid and chlorine treatment.

6.3 Irrigation scheduling

  • Based on critical stages – flowering, fruit set and fruit development.
  • Climatological approach – IW/CPE ratio, pan evaporation × crop coefficient.
  • Soil moisture – tensiometers and sensors.
  • Avoid irrigation in mango and pomegranate during the rest period before flowering (stress induces flowering).

6.4 Mulching

Mulching is covering the soil surface around plants with organic or synthetic materials.

TypeMaterialsAdvantages
OrganicStraw, sugarcane trash, dry leaves, sawdust, coir pithAdds organic matter, cools soil, cheap
Plastic (polyethylene)Black, silver-black, transparent; 25–50 µmWeed control, moisture saving, early yield; silver repels aphids/thrips
Live mulchCover crops like cowpeaProtects soil, fixes N
  • Benefits: conserves 25–50 % soil moisture, controls weeds, moderates soil temperature, prevents erosion and soil splash, improves fruit quality.
  • Transparent mulch used for soil solarisation against soil-borne pathogens.

Possible ESE questions

Short-answer questions

  1. What is fertigation? (2 marks)

    Answer points: Application of water-soluble fertilizers through the irrigation (drip) system.

  2. Define mulching. (2 marks)

    Answer points: Covering the soil surface with organic or synthetic material to conserve moisture, control weeds and moderate temperature.

  3. Name two components of a drip system. (2 marks)

    Answer points: Filter, dripper/emitter (also lateral, venturi).

Long-answer questions

  1. Describe different methods of irrigation used in horticultural crops. (10 marks)

    Answer points: Flood, basin, ring, furrow, sprinkler, drip, micro-sprinkler; suitability, efficiency, merits.

  2. Explain the components, advantages and limitations of drip irrigation. (10 marks)

    Answer points: Components list; water and fertilizer saving, yield, weed; cost, clogging, maintenance; fertigation.

  3. What is mulching? Discuss types and benefits of mulching in horticulture. (5 marks)

    Answer points: Definition; organic, plastic, live; colours; benefits; solarisation.
