The grower's hands

Besides chemicals, horticulturists use simple but powerful manual practices – pinching, disbudding, thinning, girdling, bahar treatment and bagging – to steer flowering, fruit size and quality.

24.1 Special horticultural practices

PracticeWhat is donePurpose and example
PinchingRemoving the growing tipInduces branching and more flowers – marigold, chrysanthemum, carnation
DisbuddingRemoving side budsFewer but larger flowers – rose, chrysanthemum, carnation
Deblossoming / fruit thinningRemoving some flowers or fruitsBigger, better fruits; regular bearing – grape, apple, young mango
Ringing / girdlingRemoving a ring of bark (3–5 mm)Accumulates carbohydrates above the ring – berry size in grape, fruit set
Notching and nickingCut above or below a budForces or checks bud growth
SmudgingSmoking treesTraditional flower induction in mango (Philippines, India)
Bahar treatmentWithholding water to rest the plant, then pruning, manuring and irrigationRegulates flowering season – pomegranate (ambe, mrig, hasta bahar), guava, citrus
Root pruning and bendingCutting roots / bending shootsChecks vigour, induces flowering
Desuckering, propping, bunch coveringRemoving suckers, supporting plants, bagging bunchesBanana
Fruit baggingCovering fruits with paper or non-woven bagsPest-free, blemish-free mango, pomegranate, guava
Topping and de-tassellingRemoving top or tasselsTobacco, seed production in maize

24.2 Crop regulation in guava, citrus and pomegranate

CropNatural flowering seasons (bahar)How the desired bahar is obtainedReason
GuavaAmbe (Feb–Mar), Mrig (Jun–Jul), Hasta (Oct)Withhold water and prune or apply urea spray (10–15 %) to shed unwanted flowersMrig bahar gives winter fruits of best quality
Mandarin orange (Nagpur)Ambe and MrigWater stress for 30–45 days, then manuring and irrigationAmbe bahar for higher price, Mrig for quality
PomegranateAmbe, Mrig, HastaRest, defoliation with ethrel, pruning, fertilizer and irrigationHasta bahar escapes bacterial blight; Ambe for export

Explained simply

Crop regulation is like scheduling exams: the tree is given a rest, then ‘woken up’ with water and nutrients so that it flowers when the market and weather are best.

Facts & amazing information

1

Pomegranate in Maharashtra is often given ‘hasta bahar’ (Sept–Oct flowering) to escape bacterial blight of the rainy season.

2

Girdling grape canes at berry set can increase berry weight by 10–20 %.

3

Bagged mangoes and pomegranates fetch premium prices because they are free from fruit fly marks and pesticide residues.

Long-answer questions for ESE

Each question shows marks, Bloom's Taxonomy Level (BTL) and the course outcome it assesses.

  1. Describe special horticultural practices such as pinching, disbudding, girdling and bahar treatment, and apply them to improve flower and fruit production. (10 marks · BTL-3 Apply · CO3)

    Answer points: Definitions, procedure, purpose; examples – marigold, rose, grape, pomegranate; benefits.

  2. Design a bahar-treatment schedule for pomegranate to obtain fruits in the summer market while avoiding bacterial blight. (10 marks · BTL-6 Create · CO3)

    Answer points: Choice of bahar; rest period, defoliation with ethrel, pruning, fertilizer and irrigation schedule; disease escape; marketing.

  3. Evaluate the role of fruit thinning, deblossoming and bagging in producing export-quality fruits. (5 marks · BTL-5 Evaluate · CO3)

    Answer points: Crop-load regulation, fruit size, regular bearing, protection from pests and sunburn, residue-free fruit.
