Back to nature – with science

Many consumers today pay more for food grown without synthetic chemicals. Organic and natural farming try to grow healthy food by feeding the soil life rather than the plant directly.

5.1 Organic farming

  • A production system that avoids synthetic fertilizers, pesticides, GMOs and growth regulators, relying on crop rotation, organic manures, biofertilizers and biological pest control.
  • Certification: NPOP (third-party, for export) and PGS-India (participatory, for domestic market); logos ‘India Organic’ and ‘Jaivik Bharat’.
  • Schemes: Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY); Sikkim became India's first fully organic state (2016).

The National Mission on Natural Farming (2024) promotes this low-cost system across India.

5.3 Strengths and concerns

  • Strengths: lower input cost, better soil health, premium price, safer food.
  • Concerns: yield dip in the first 2–3 years, certification costs, need for scientific validation and market linkage.

Long-answer questions for ESE

  1. Explain the preparation and use of jeevamrut and beejamrut in natural farming. (5 marks · BTL-2 Understand · CO4)

    Answer points: Ingredients, fermentation, application; claimed effects; scientific evidence.

  2. Analyse the yield and soil-health evidence from long-term organic and natural farming trials in India. (7 marks · BTL-4 Analyse · CO4)

    Answer points: Yield gap in transition, soil OC, biology; variability; research needs.

  3. Explain the certification systems for organic produce in India and analyse their relevance to small farmers. (8 marks · BTL-4 Analyse · CO4)

    Answer points: NPOP, PGS-India; procedures; costs; group certification.

  4. Differentiate organic and natural farming and critically evaluate their potential to ensure food security in India. (10 marks · BTL-5 Evaluate · CO4)

    Answer points: Principles, inputs, certification; yields; soil health; markets; limitations.

  5. Design a three-year conversion plan from conventional to organic vegetable production. (12 marks · BTL-6 Create · CO4)

    Answer points: Soil building, inputs, pest management, records, certification, marketing.
