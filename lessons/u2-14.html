Putting the seed in the ground

Once the season is chosen, the next questions are how to sow, how much seed to use and how to protect it. The answers decide the plant population, and plant population is the first building block of yield.

14.1 Methods of sowing

MethodProcedureAdvantagesLimitations / crops
BroadcastingSeed scattered by hand and covered with harrow or plankQuick, cheapUneven stand, more seed, weeding difficult – fodder, sesame
DibblingSeed placed in holes at fixed spacingLess seed, uniformSlow, laborious – cotton, castor, groundnut
Drilling / line sowingSeed dropped in rows by seed drill, tifan or seed-cum-fertilizer drillUniform depth, easy intercultivationNeeds machinery – wheat, soybean, gram
TransplantingSeedlings raised in a nursery and planted in the main fieldSaves field time, uniform stand, less seedLabour intensive – rice, onion, tomato, chilli
Planting of vegetative partsSetts, tubers, rhizomes plantedTrue-to-type cropSugarcane, potato, turmeric
Broad bed and furrow (BBF) / ridge and furrowSeed on raised bedsDrainage and in-situ moisture conservationSoybean, chickpea, cotton
Zero-till / happy seederDirect sowing into residue without ploughingSaves time and cost, no residue burningWheat after rice
Direct-seeded rice (DSR)Dry or wet seed sown directlySaves 25–30 % water and labourNeeds weed control

14.2 Seed rate and plant population

Seed rate (kg/ha) = (Plant population per ha × 1000-seed weight in g) ÷ (Germination % × Purity % × 100). Plant population = 10,000 m² ÷ area per plant.

Worked example

Wheat at 22.5 cm rows with about 2.25 million plants/ha, 1000-seed weight 40 g, germination 90 % and purity 100 % → 2,250,000 × 40 ÷ (90 × 100 × 100) = 100 kg/ha.

14.3 Seed treatment

Seed treatment is the application of fungicides, insecticides, bio-agents, biofertilizers or nutrients to seed before sowing to protect it from seed- and soil-borne pests and pathogens and to improve germination and early vigour.

TypeMaterial and dose per kg seedPurpose
Chemical fungicideThiram 3 g, carbendazim 2 g, carboxin + thiram 2.5 gSeed rot, damping-off, smuts, wilt
Bio-agentTrichoderma viride/harzianum 4–10 gWilt and root rot of chickpea, soybean, cotton
BiofertilizersRhizobium, PSB, KSB 25 g (or 10 ml liquid)N fixation, P and K solubilisation in legumes
Azotobacter / Azospirillum25 gN fixation in cereals and millets
InsecticideImidacloprid or thiamethoxam as per labelTermites, sucking pests
PhysicalHot water 52 °C for 30 min; solar heat; moist hot air for sugarcane settsLoose smut of wheat, grassy shoot of sugarcane
Seed priming / hardeningSoak in water, KNO3 or CaCl2 and dry backFast, uniform emergence under dryland
Seed pelleting / coatingSeed coated with nutrients, lime or polymerBetter establishment in acid soils
Fungicide➜Insecticide➜Rhizobium / biofertilizer (last)➜Dry in shade➜Sow within 24 h

Key point

Follow the FIR order – Fungicide, Insecticide, then Rhizobium. Never mix biofertilizers with chemicals, and never dry treated seed in direct sun.

Facts & amazing information

1

Seed treatment costs less than ₹100 per hectare yet can prevent losses worth thousands of rupees.

2

A single sugarcane bud chip can produce a healthy seedling and saves about 80 % of seed cane.

3

Direct-seeded rice needs 80–100 kg seed/ha, whereas transplanted rice needs only about 40 kg.

4

Happy Seeder technology lets Punjab farmers sow wheat without burning rice straw.

Long-answer questions for ESE

  1. Critically compare the methods of sowing of field crops and recommend a suitable method for soybean, wheat and rice with justification. (10 marks · BTL-5 Evaluate · CO2)

    Answer points: Broadcasting, dibbling, drilling, transplanting, vegetative planting, BBF, zero-till, DSR; merits and limits; recommendation – soybean BBF, wheat seed drill, rice transplanting/DSR.

  2. Calculate the seed rate of chickpea for a spacing of 30 × 10 cm when the 100-seed weight is 22 g, germination is 85 % and purity is 98 %, and explain each step. (5 marks · BTL-3 Apply · CO2)

    Answer points: Population = 10,000 ÷ 0.03 = 3,33,333 plants/ha; 1000-seed weight 220 g; seed rate = 3,33,333 × 220 ÷ (85 × 98 × 100) ≈ 88 kg/ha; steps explained.

  3. Explain the types of seed treatment and design a complete seed-treatment schedule for chickpea following the FIR principle. (10 marks · BTL-6 Create · CO2)

    Answer points: Chemical, biological, biofertilizer, physical, priming, pelleting; chickpea – thiram + carbendazim, then insecticide if termites, then Rhizobium + PSB + Trichoderma; doses; precautions.

  4. Evaluate conservation-oriented sowing methods such as zero tillage, BBF and DSR for sustainable crop production in India. (10 marks · BTL-5 Evaluate · CO2)

    Answer points: Principles, machinery, benefits – cost, water, residue, soil health; limitations – weeds, skills; suitable regions; evidence.
