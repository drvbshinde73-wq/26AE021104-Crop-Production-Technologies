An orchard is planned for 50 years

A field crop lasts four months; a mango orchard lasts 50 years or more. A mistake in site, layout or planting cannot be corrected easily. That is why orchard establishment is planned with great care.

3.1 Site selection

  • Climate suited to the fruit – temperature, rainfall, frost, hot winds.
  • Soil – deep (≥ 1.5 m), well-drained, pH 6.5–7.5, free from hardpan and salts.
  • Water – assured irrigation source and good quality water.
  • Topography – level or gentle slope; terraces on hills.
  • Transport and market – roads, cold storage, processing units nearby.
  • Labour and electricity availability; no neighbouring pollution.

3.2 Planning the orchard

  • Fencing (live fence – Agave, Duranta) and windbreaks of tall trees (casuarina, silver oak) on the windward side.
  • Roads, irrigation channels/drip mainlines, store, packing shed.
  • Tall trees at the back, evergreen and deciduous separately; pollinizer varieties where needed.

3.4 Planting methods

Mark layout➜Dig pits 1 × 1 × 1 m➜Sun-dry pits 15 days➜Fill topsoil + FYM + SSP + neem cake➜Plant graft at centre➜Stake & irrigate
  • Planting at the onset of monsoon (June–July) in rainfed areas; Feb–March in irrigated areas.
  • Graft union must be 15–20 cm above soil; do not bury it.
  • Use a planting board to place the plant exactly at the pit centre.
  • Provide shade and protection from termites; mulching around the basin.

Explained simply

A pit 1 m deep is like a baby's cradle – soft, rich soil lets the young roots spread fast in the first years.

3.5 Intercropping in young orchards

Short-duration vegetables, legumes or fillers (papaya, guava) are grown between young fruit trees for 3–5 years to earn income, suppress weeds and improve soil. They must not compete heavily with the main trees.

Facts & amazing information

1

The hexagonal system accommodates about 15 % more trees than the square system.

2

Ultra-high-density mango orchards in Gujarat and Maharashtra plant over 600 trees/ha.

3

A windbreak protects an area up to about 10–15 times its height on the leeward side.

4

Maharashtra's horticulture plantation scheme made the state a leader in fruit-crop area.

Possible ESE questions

Short-answer questions

  1. What is the quincunx system? (2 marks)

    Answer points: Square system with an extra (filler) plant at the centre of each square.

  2. Calculate plants/ha for mango at 10 × 10 m. (2 marks)

    Answer points: 10,000 ÷ 100 = 100 plants/ha.

  3. Why should the graft union be above soil? (2 marks)

    Answer points: To prevent scion rooting and collar rot and keep rootstock effect.

Long-answer questions

  1. Explain the factors considered for site selection of an orchard. (5 marks)

    Answer points: Climate, soil, water, topography, market and transport, labour, power, pollution.

  2. Describe the different systems of orchard layout with diagrams. (10 marks)

    Answer points: Square, rectangle, quincunx, hexagonal, contour, HDP; formulae, diagrams, merits.

  3. Explain the method of planting fruit plants in a new orchard. (5 marks)

    Answer points: Pit size, filling mixture, time of planting, planting board, graft union, staking, irrigation, care.
