Tiny amounts, huge effects

A spray of only a few milligrams per litre can make a mango tree flower every year, keep a grape bunch loose, or make a cutting root in two weeks. These magic molecules are plant growth regulators (PGRs).

4.1 Definitions

Plant hormones are organic substances produced naturally in plants that in very low concentrations regulate growth and development. Plant growth regulators are natural or synthetic compounds that, applied in small amounts, modify plant growth.

4.2 Classes of PGRs and their uses

GroupExamplesHorticultural uses
AuxinsIAA, IBA, NAA, 2,4-DRooting of cuttings (IBA 2000–5000 ppm), prevent fruit drop (NAA 20 ppm in mango), fruit thinning
GibberellinsGA3Berry elongation and seedless grapes, break seed dormancy, increase fruit size
CytokininsKinetin, BA, CPPUCell division, tissue culture shoot formation, berry size in grapes
EthyleneEthephon (ethrel)Ripening of banana and mango, flower induction in pineapple, degreening citrus
Abscisic acid (ABA)ABADormancy, stress tolerance, colour development in grapes
Growth retardantsPaclobutrazol (cultar), CCC, daminozideRegular flowering in mango, dwarfing, compact pot plants

Remember

Auxin = roots and fruit-set, Gibberellin = grow bigger/longer, Cytokinin = divide cells, Ethylene = ripen, ABA = rest and stress.

Facts & amazing information

1

Thompson Seedless grapes owe their long, large berries to GA3 dips developed by Indian scientists.

2

Soil drench of paclobutrazol can make ‘off-year’ Alphonso mango trees flower regularly.

3

Ethylene is the only plant hormone that is a gas.

4

Pomegranate in Maharashtra is given ‘hasta bahar’ (Sept–Oct flowering) to escape bacterial blight in the rainy season.

Possible ESE questions

Short-answer questions

  1. Define plant growth regulator. (2 marks)

    Answer points: Natural or synthetic compound that in small amounts modifies plant growth and development.

  2. Which PGR is used for rooting of cuttings? (2 marks)

    Answer points: IBA (indole-3-butyric acid), also NAA.

  3. What is pinching? (2 marks)

    Answer points: Removal of terminal growing tip to induce branching.

Long-answer questions

  1. Classify plant growth regulators and give their uses in horticulture. (10 marks)

    Answer points: Auxins, GA, cytokinins, ethylene, ABA, retardants – examples, concentrations, uses in fruits, vegetables and flowers.

  2. Explain special horticultural practices with examples. (10 marks)

    Answer points: Pinching, disbudding, thinning, girdling, notching, smudging, bahar treatment, root pruning, desuckering, bagging.

  3. What is bahar treatment? Explain its importance in pomegranate. (5 marks)

    Answer points: Ambe, mrig, hasta bahar; water stress, defoliation, pruning, irrigation and fertilizer; choice of bahar for market and disease escape.
