This answer sheet puts the complete package of practices (POP) of all 7 field crops (Unit II, Lectures 17–18) and all 10 horticultural crops (Unit III, Lectures 28–30) side by side. Each column is one crop; each row is one practice – botany, climate, soil, season, varieties, land preparation, seed, nutrients, water, weeds, pests, diseases, disorders, harvest, yield and post-harvest. Use the tabs: Complete table shows both sections together (Section A – field crops, Section B – horticultural crops); each crop tab shows that crop alone. On a phone, swipe the big table sideways; the practice names stay fixed on the left.

Recommendations follow the Maharashtra state agricultural universities (MPKV Rahuri, Dr PDKV Akola, VNMKV Parbhani, Dr BSKKV Dapoli) and ICAR institutes; always check the latest university package before field use. Abbreviations: DAS/DAT – days after sowing/transplanting; CRI – crown-root initiation; PI – panicle initiation; ETL – economic threshold level; a.i. – active ingredient; BBF – broad bed and furrow; HDPS – high-density planting system.

  • Rainfed: after 75–100 mm rain (end June – 1st week July)
  • Irrigated: 1st week of June
  • pre-monsoon (May) sowing discouraged – favours pink bollworm
Improved varieties / hybrids
  • Bt hybrids (Bollgard II, Cry1Ac + Cry2Ab) – > 90 % area
  • Public: NHH 44, PKV Hy 2, Phule 688, AKH 081, NH 615, Suraj
  • Desi: PKV Suvarna, AKA 7, PA 255, PA 740, JLA 505
  • Compact (HDPS): PKV 081, NH 615, Suraj
Land preparation & layout
  • Deep ploughing every 2–3 years + 2–3 harrowings
  • remove previous stubble (pink bollworm)
  • Ridges & furrows or BBF
  • paired rows with drip lateral
Seed rate / planting material & spacing
  • Bt hybrid 2.5 kg/ha (475 g packet/acre)
  • varieties 8–10 kg/ha (delinted)
  • Rainfed 90 × 60 or 120 × 30 cm
  • Irrigated 120 × 60 or 150 × 30 cm
  • HDPS 90 × 15 cm (compact types)
  • Dibbling 2 seeds/hill
Seed / sett treatment
  • Acid-delinting (conc. H₂SO₄ 100 mL/kg) for varieties
  • Bt seed comes pre-treated
  • Azotobacter + PSB
  • Imidacloprid 7–10 g/kg against sucking pests
Manures & fertilisers (kg N : P₂O₅ : K₂O/ha)
  • FYM 10 t/ha (irrigated), 5 t/ha (rainfed)
  • Bt rainfed 100:50:50
  • irrigated 120:60:60 (N in 3 splits – sowing, 30, 60 DAS)
  • MgSO₄ 20–25, ZnSO₄ 20, borax 1 kg B/ha
  • 2 % DAP or 1 % KNO₃ spray at flowering & boll development
Irrigation & critical stages
  • Critical: flowering & boll development (most), sowing, square formation
  • Drip + fertigation raises yield 30–40 %, saves 40–50 % water
  • Stop when 50 % bolls open
  • avoid stagnation
Weed management
  • Critical period: 15–60 DAS
  • Pre-emergence pendimethalin 1.0 kg a.i./ha
  • Post-emergence pyrithiobac-Na 62.5 g + quizalofop 50 g/ha at 20–25 DAS
  • 2–3 hoeings (dawra) + hand weeding
Intercultural / special practices
  • Thinning + gap filling 10–15 DAS
  • Hoeing + earthing up 30–35 DAS
  • Detopping at 80–90 DAS (15–20 sympodia)
  • Mepiquat chloride (HDPS)
  • NAA 20 ppm against square shedding
  • Refuge rows (non-Bt) around Bt crop
  • Intercrop cotton + tur / soybean / moong
Major pests & management (IPM)
  • Pink bollworm (now Bt-resistant) – rosette flowers, damaged seed: timely sowing, short-duration hybrids, end crop by Dec, pheromone traps 5/ha, destroy stubble, profenophos/emamectin at ETL (8 moths/trap/night for 3 nights or 10 % damaged bolls)
  • Sucking pests – jassids, aphids, thrips, whitefly: yellow sticky traps, flonicamid/ imidacloprid, conserve Chrysoperla
  • American bollworm, mealybug
Major diseases & management (IDM)
  • Bacterial blight / black arm (Xanthomonas) – angular spots, black stem lesions: acid-delinted seed, copper oxychloride 2.5 g/L + streptocycline 0.1 g/L
  • Alternaria leaf spot – concentric rings: mancozeb/ propiconazole
  • Grey mildew (Ramularia) – white powder on lower leaf: carbendazim / wettable sulphur
  • Root rot, boll rot, leaf curl virus (north)
Physiological disorders
  • Leaf reddening – Mg, N deficiency, stress, cold nights: 1 % MgSO₄ + 2 % urea spray
  • Parawilt (sudden wilt) after heavy rain/irrigation following drought: cobalt chloride 10 ppm within 24–48 h, drainage
  • Square & boll shedding – stress, excess rain: NAA 20 ppm, irrigation
Maturity indices & harvesting
  • Bolls burst, lint fluffy and dry
  • 150–180 days (Bt)
  • Hand picking at 7–10-day intervals, 3–5 pickings, after dew dries (after 10 a.m.)
  • keep first & last pickings separate
  • avoid trash, plastic contamination
Yield
  • Rainfed Bt 15–20 q kapas/ha
  • irrigated 25–35 q/ha
  • Ginning out-turn (lint %) 33–36 %
Post-harvest & by-products
  • Dry kapas in shade to 8–10 %
  • grade by colour, trash
  • store dry (no plastic bags)
  • Ginning → lint + seed
  • bales of 170 kg
  • Seed oil (gossypol removed), cake (feed), linters, stalk (fuel, particle board)
  • Shred/uproot stalks after last picking (break pink bollworm cycle)
