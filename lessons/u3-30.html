Golden spice and fragrant herbs

Turmeric gives colour, flavour and medicine to Indian food; tulsi is the sacred and medicinal basil of every Indian home; lemongrass yields an essential oil prized by the perfume and pharmaceutical industries. They are high-value crops that suit small farms and value addition.

30.1 Turmeric (Curcuma longa L.)

PracticeRecommendation
Climate and soilWarm humid; 1,500 mm rain or irrigation; well-drained sandy loam to medium black soil rich in organic matter
VarietiesSalem, Rajapuri (Sangli), Krishna, Phule Swaroopa, Pragati (IISR), Prathiba
Planting material and timeMother or finger rhizomes, 25 q/ha; treat with carbendazim + quinalphos or Trichoderma; plant May–June
Planting systemRaised beds 1 m wide with two rows; 30 × 15 cm or 45 × 15 cm; mulch with green leaves
NutrientsFYM 25 t/ha; 200 : 100 : 100 kg N : P₂O₅ : K₂O/ha; N and K in splits; earthing up at 60 and 90 days
ProblemsRhizome rot, leaf spot, leaf blotch; shoot borer, rhizome scale
Harvest and yield8–9 months, when leaves dry; 300–350 q/ha fresh rhizomes (≈ 20–25 % dry recovery)

Post-harvest processing

Cleaning➜Boiling 45–60 min➜Sun-drying 10–15 days (≈ 10 % moisture)➜Polishing➜Grading➜Storage / grinding

Key point

Curcumin content (2–7 %) decides the market value of turmeric; varieties such as Pragati and Prathiba are rich in curcumin.

30.2 Tulsi (Ocimum tenuiflorum L.)

PracticeRecommendation
Climate and soilTropical and subtropical; hardy; well-drained light to medium soils
Types and varietiesRama and Krishna tulsi; CIM-Ayu, CIM-Angana (CSIR-CIMAP)
PropagationSeed nursery; 6-week seedlings transplanted
Spacing45 × 45 cm
NutrientsFYM 15 t/ha; 120 : 60 : 60 kg/ha
HarvestLeaves and tops at full bloom, 90–95 days after transplanting; 2–3 harvests a year
ProductsDry leaves, herbal teas and essential oil (eugenol, methyl eugenol); used widely in Ayurveda
YieldFresh herbage 20–25 t/ha

30.3 Lemongrass (Cymbopogon flexuosus)

PracticeRecommendation
Climate and soilWarm humid; grows even on poor, sloping and marginal soils
VarietiesKrishna, OD-19, CIM-Shikhar, Pragati
PropagationRooted slips; 60 × 45 cm
NutrientsFYM + 150 : 60 : 60 kg/ha/year in splits after each cutting
HarvestFirst cutting 4–5 months after planting, then 4–6 cuttings a year for 5–6 years
OilSteam distillation of fresh or partly wilted leaves; oil recovery 0.5–0.8 %; citral 75–90 %
YieldOil 100–150 kg/ha/year

Facts & amazing information

India produces about 75–80 % of the world's turmeric.

2

Sangli is one of Asia's largest turmeric trading markets.

3

Citral from lemongrass oil was once the key raw material for synthetic vitamin A.

4

Eugenol from tulsi gives it its clove-like aroma and antimicrobial action.

Long-answer questions for ESE

Each question shows marks, Bloom's Taxonomy Level (BTL) and the course outcome it assesses.

  1. Formulate a complete package of practices for turmeric on raised beds and evaluate the post-harvest processing steps that decide its market quality. (10 marks · BTL-6 Create · CO3)

    Answer points: Variety, rhizome treatment, beds, spacing, nutrients, mulch, earthing up, IPM, harvest; boiling, drying, polishing, curcumin, grading.

  2. Analyse the scope of medicinal and aromatic plants such as tulsi and lemongrass for small farmers in Maharashtra. (10 marks · BTL-4 Analyse · CO3)

    Answer points: Low input, marginal land, multiple harvests, distillation units, buy-back, markets, risks.

  3. Describe the principle of steam distillation with a diagram and calculate oil recovery when 500 kg of lemongrass yields 3.5 kg of oil. (10 marks · BTL-3 Apply · CO3)

    Answer points: Steam carries volatile oil, condensation, separation; recovery = 3.5/500 × 100 = 0.7 %.

  4. Compare the harvesting and processing of tulsi and lemongrass for leaf and oil production. (5 marks · BTL-4 Analyse · CO3)

    Answer points: Stage of harvest, frequency, drying vs distillation, quality constituents (eugenol, citral).

  5. Explain the importance of curcumin content and variety choice in turmeric marketing. (5 marks · BTL-2 Understand · CO3)

    Answer points: Curcumin %, high-curcumin varieties, export standards, price premium.
