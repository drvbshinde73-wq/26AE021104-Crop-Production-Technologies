The right seed, at the right time, in the right way

Ask any experienced farmer in Maharashtra and he will say: “A crop sown on time is half harvested.” The crop season, the sowing method and the health of the seed decide how many plants stand in the field – and plant stand decides yield.

3.3 Seed rate and plant population

Seed rate (kg/ha) = (Plant population/ha × 1000-seed weight in g) ÷ (Germination % × Purity % × 100). Plant population comes from spacing: 10,000 m² ÷ area per plant.

Worked example

Example: wheat at 22.5 cm rows, 2.25 million plants/ha, 1000-seed weight 40 g and 90 % germination needs ≈ 100 kg seed/ha.

3.4 Seed treatment

Seed treatment is the application of fungicides, insecticides, bio-agents or nutrients to seed to protect it from seed- and soil-borne pests and diseases and to improve germination.

TypeMaterial (dose per kg seed)Purpose
FungicideThiram 3 g or carbendazim 2 gSeed rot, damping-off, smut
Bio-agentTrichoderma viride 4–10 gWilt and root rot (chickpea, soybean)
BiofertilizerRhizobium, PSB 25 g (after fungicide)N fixation and P solubilisation in legumes
Azotobacter / Azospirillum25 gN fixation in cereals
InsecticideImidacloprid / thiamethoxam as labelSucking pests, termites
PhysicalHot water (52 °C, 30 min), solar treatmentLoose smut of wheat, setts of sugarcane
Seed primingSoaking in water/KNO3 and dryingFast, uniform emergence in dryland

FIR order: Fungicide → Insecticide → Rhizobium/biofertilizer (biofertilizer always last, dried in shade).

Facts & amazing information

1

Sowing wheat after 15 December can reduce yield by 30–40 kg/ha for every day of delay.

2

Transplanted rice needs only about 40 kg seed/ha, but direct-seeded rice needs 80–100 kg.

3

Seed treatment costs less than ₹100/ha yet can prevent losses of thousands of rupees.

4

A single sugarcane bud chip can grow a healthy seedling – it saves about 80 % of the seed cane.

Possible ESE questions

Short-answer questions

  1. What is dibbling? (2 marks)

    Answer points: Placing seeds in holes made at fixed spacing, e.g. cotton, castor.

  2. Define seed treatment. (2 marks)

    Answer points: Application of chemicals, bio-agents or nutrients to seed to protect it and improve germination.

  3. What is the FIR order in seed treatment? (2 marks)

    Answer points: Fungicide first, then Insecticide, then Rhizobium/biofertilizer.

Long-answer questions

  1. Describe the crop seasons of India with examples. (5 marks)

    Answer points: Kharif, rabi, zaid – periods, weather, crops; Maharashtra sugarcane seasons.

  2. Explain various methods of sowing with their merits and demerits. (10 marks)

    Answer points: Broadcasting, dibbling, drilling, seed-cum-fertilizer drill, transplanting, planting, BBF/ridge-furrow, zero-till; table of merits.

  3. What is seed treatment? Explain its types, materials and advantages. (10 marks)

    Answer points: Definition; fungicide, insecticide, biofertilizer, bio-agent, physical, priming; doses; FIR order; advantages.
